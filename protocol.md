# CRISPR Knockout Screening Protocol

**Status:** Draft V3

## Overview
Protocol for generating single-gene knockouts in HEK293 cells using
CRISPR-Cas9 ribonucleoprotein (RNP) delivery, for downstream phenotypic
screening. (Gold Standard method)

## Reagents
- Cas9 protein (NLS-tagged)
- Synthetic sgRNA (target-specific)
- Electroporation buffer
- HEK293 cells, passage < 20
- Non targeting-control sgRNA
-Cell viability dye(for example trypan blue)

## Steps
Phase 1: sgRNA Design & RNP Assembly
1. Design 2-3 chemically modified syntehtic sgRNA and order targeting exon 2 of the gene of interest. 'Avoid the immediate N-terminus'
2. Assemble RNP Complexes (Per Well of a 24-Well Plate), Complex Cas9 protein with sgRNA at a 1:1.2 molar ratio, incubate 10 min RT.
- Combine 1.5 µL of sgRNA (4.5 pmol total; if using a 3-guide pool, use 1.5 pmol of each) with 1.5 µL of Cas9 protein (4.5 pmol) at a 1:1 to 1.5:1 molar ratio.
- Bring the total volume to 25 µL with Opti-MEM I Reduced Serum Medium.
- Mix gently by pipetting (do not vortex) and incubate at room temperature for 10–15 minutes to form active RNPs

Phase 2: RNP Delivery into HEK293 Cells
3. Electroporate RNP complex into 2x10^5 cells per reaction.
4. Recover cells in antibiotic-free media for 48h.

Phase 3: Genotyping & Knockdown Validation
5. Validate editing efficiency by amplicon sequencing.
